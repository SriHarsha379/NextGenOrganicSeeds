About Winter Flower Seeds

Winter is the most rewarding season for flower gardening in India. Cool temperatures between October and February create perfect conditions for a wide range of flowering plants — producing longer-lasting, more vibrant blooms than in warmer months.

Hasafarm's winter flower seed collection includes marigold, pansy, petunia, alyssum, larkspur, calendula, dianthus, and many more — all tested for germination and vibrancy in Indian conditions.

When to Sow Winter Flower Seeds in India?

The best time to sow winter flower seeds in India is September to November. Seeds germinate in 7–15 days and plants begin flowering from November through February.

When should I sow winter flower seeds in India?
The ideal sowing time is September to November. This ensures blooming from November through February during India's cool season.
Which winter flowers are easiest to grow in pots?
Marigold, petunia, pansy, and alyssum are among the easiest for pot cultivation. They need minimal care and produce abundant blooms throughout the season.
How much water do winter flower plants need?
Winter flowers need moderate watering — once every 2 days or when the topsoil feels dry. Ensure good drainage to prevent root rot.
Can winter flower seeds be directly sown outdoors?
Yes, most winter flower seeds can be directly sown in garden beds or pots. Starting in seedling trays and transplanting after 3–4 weeks can improve success rates.
