Shipping & Delivery Policy

We deliver fresh, carefully packed seeds to every corner of India. Here's everything you need to know about our shipping process.

Delivery Timelines

  • Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of placement (on working days).
  • Standard delivery across India takes 5–10 working days from dispatch.
  • Remote or hilly areas may experience slightly longer delivery times.

Order Tracking

  • Tracking details (courier name + tracking number) are shared via email and SMS after dispatch.
  • You can use the tracking number on the courier partner's website to check live status.
  • If you have not received tracking details within 48 hours, please contact us.

Packaging

  • All seeds are packed in moisture-proof, sealed packets to preserve freshness and viability.
  • Orders are packed securely in tamper-proof packaging to prevent damage during transit.

Courier Delays

  • Hasafarm is not responsible for delays caused by courier partners, natural events, strikes, or other factors beyond our control.
  • If your order is delayed beyond 15 days, please contact us and we will assist in tracking or re-shipping.

Incorrect Address

  • Please double-check your delivery address before placing an order.
  • Re-delivery fees will apply if a delivery fails due to an incorrect or incomplete address provided by the customer.
  • Address changes after dispatch are not guaranteed and depend on the courier partner.
Shipping queries? Contact us at contact@hasafarm.com with your order ID and we'll help track your order.
